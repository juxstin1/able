# Air and transition FX options

12 original stereo 48 kHz / 24-bit WAVs at **123.70 BPM**.

Drag individual WAVs into Arrangement. They are rendered to exact beat lengths (within one sample); keep Warp off at this tempo.
Risers and reverse swells: align the RIGHT edge with the transition. Downlifters and impacts: align the LEFT edge with the transition.
The halo is B minor; the other air effects are unpitched. The bloom impact has a short downward-pitched low thump.
Individual peaks are between -9 and -12 dBFS. Start quietly in a full mix and bring up to taste.

## In the open Ableton set

The options begin at **bar 97**, after the existing arrangement, across four labelled FX Options tracks. They play one at a time in numeric order with a one-bar gap. Existing song loop range was retained. All 12 clip lengths were read back from Live with Warp off.

Drag a clip from this palette into the song, or hold Ctrl while dragging to leave the original option in place.
